Dear friends,

It's been three years since our last production. After the success of Things I Know to Be True, it felt like the right time to step away for a while. We didn't want to come back until we had something truly special to share with you.

Jackpot Twins is that show.

For nearly two decades, The Company Theatre has been focused on bringing Toronto the best international plays we could find — Jerusalem, Festen, John, Belleville, Domesticated. But in today's world, it felt like time to turn inward and tell a Canadian story. Jackpot Twins is the first original work we've ever produced. Our first world premiere. And we wanted it to be something entertaining, funny, and full of heart.

Our late founding member Diane Mugford used to ask me all the time: "When are we going to produce a comedy?"

I was always a little confused by this. I thought we had — Jerusalem, The Test, John, Domesticated, even Festen. They were, on some level, funny. But even I have to admit: they were mostly dramas — tragedies, even — that happened to have laughs in them.

Jackpot Twins is a comedy. One with heart and depth, but a comedy. It's something different from us. I'm sure Diane is smiling down right now, thinking: "Finally."

We are still the same Company Theatre — obsessed with theatre that is radically alive, with a cast made up of some of Canada's finest actors. To find out who, you'll have to visit jackpottwins.ca.

We're thrilled to be continuing our partnership with Mirvish Productions, who once again have been extraordinary collaborators. After Things I Know to Be True at the CAA Theatre in 2023, this felt like a natural next step — and we're grateful to David and Hannah Mirvish for believing in this story.

Tickets are not yet on sale — but we'll be in touch the moment they are. In the meantime, visit jackpottwins.ca to learn more about the show and the incredible cast bringing it to life.

We hope you missed us as much as we missed you. I hope this one will be worth the wait.

Sincerely,

Philip Riccio

Artistic Director, The Company Theatre
