Winning the lottery once would change your life. Winning it three times? That’s where things get interesting.

Jackpot Twins is our new Canadian comedy about impossible good fortune—and the very human complications that come with it.

With Seana McKenna and Nora McLellan leading an extraordinary cast, we can’t wait to share this world premiere with you.

See you at the theatre,
Philip
