The Company Theatre

Dear friends,

It's been a while. How the heck have you been?

You've probably been busy living your life — and you may not have noticed — but after our last show Things I Know to Be True in 2023, we decided to take a little hiatus. We vowed not to come back until we had something truly special to share with you.

I couldn't be more thrilled that our hiatus is officially over and we will be back in 2027 to bring you the World Premiere of Jackpot Twins.

For nearly two decades, The Company Theatre has been focused on bringing Toronto the best international plays we could find. But in today's world, it felt like time to turn inward and tell a Canadian story. Jackpot Twins is the first original work we've ever produced. Our first world premiere. And our first true comedy.

Our late founding member Diane Mugford used to ask me all the time: "When are we going to produce a comedy?"

I was always a little confused by this. I thought we had — Jerusalem, The Test, John, Domesticated, even Festen. I thought they were all very funny. But I suppose even I have to admit: they were at their core dramas — tragedies, even — that happened to have laughs in them.

Jackpot Twins is a comedy. One with heart and depth and hopefully with themes that will be thought provoking and spiritually stimulating — but a comedy. It's something a little different from us. I'm sure Diane is smiling down right now, thinking: "Finally."

We are still the same Company Theatre — obsessed with theatre that is visceral and radically live, with a cast made up of some of Canada's finest actors. (To find out who, you'll have to visit jackpottwins.ca.)

This upcoming production is also the continuation of our artistic collaboration with Mirvish Productions. After the experience of working with them on Things I Know to Be True in 2023, they are beginning to feel like our new artistic home — and we're grateful to David and Hannah Mirvish for believing in this new play.

Single tickets are not yet on sale — but we'll be in touch the moment they are. In the meantime, visit jackpottwins.ca to learn more about the show and the incredible cast that will be bringing it to life.

We hope you missed us as much as we missed you. I hope this one will be worth the wait.

Sincerely,

Philip Riccio

Artistic Director, The Company Theatre
