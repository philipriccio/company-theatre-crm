# Jackpot Twins Announcement Email — Draft v2

**Subject line options:**
1. We're back.
2. It's been a while.
3. Something new from The Company Theatre

**Preview text:** After three years, we have something special to share.

---

Dear friends,

It's been three years since our last production. After the success of *Things I Know to Be True*, it felt like the right time to step away for a while. We didn't want to come back until we had something truly special to share with you.

*Jackpot Twins* is that show.

For nearly two decades, The Company Theatre has been focused on bringing Toronto the best international plays we could find — *Jerusalem*, *Festen*, *John*, *Belleville*, *Domesticated*. But in today's world, it felt like time to turn inward and tell a Canadian story. *Jackpot Twins* is the first original work we've ever produced. Our first world premiere. And we wanted it to be something entertaining, funny, and full of heart.

Our late founding member Diane Mugford used to ask me all the time: *"When are we going to produce a comedy?"*

I was always a little confused by this. I thought we *had* produced comedies — *Jerusalem*, *The Test*, *John*, *Domesticated*, even *Festen*. I always thought they were, on some level, funny. But even I have to admit: they were mostly dramas — even tragedies — that happened to have laughs in them.

*Jackpot Twins* is a comedy. One with heart and depth, but a comedy. It's something different from us. I'm sure Diane is smiling down right now, thinking: *"Finally."*

We are still the same Company Theatre — obsessed with theatre that is radically alive, with a cast made up of some of Canada's finest actors. (To find out who, you'll have to visit [jackpottwins.ca](https://jackpottwins.ca).)

We're also thrilled to be continuing our partnership with Mirvish Productions, who once again have been extraordinary collaborators. After *Things I Know to Be True* at the CAA Theatre in 2023, this felt like a natural next step — and we're grateful to David and Hannah Mirvish for believing in this story.

We hope you missed us as much as we missed you. And I hope this production will be worth the wait.

Sincerely,
Philip Riccio
Artistic Director, The Company Theatre
